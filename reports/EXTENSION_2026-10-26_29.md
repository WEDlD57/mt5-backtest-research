## What this period can establish

These dates were previously unbacktested in this research. They are an **unexamined historical extension**, not wholly prospective validation: the source was frozen October 28, while part of this window precedes that date. The extension plan was recorded October 30, after the price period existed, before its return evaluation. It is not a preregistration before data collection. Positions, balance and armed-retest state were reset for this separate test; its return must not be compounded with prior independent runs.

The execution check passed. Quote tracing recorded 5,460 minute aggregates and 2,048,063 ticks across all four days. Every minute from 07:00 through 20:00 was covered on each day, including the exit deadline. Both native reports showed 100% history quality. The logs contained no detected real-tick fallback warning, zero indicator warm-up skips, zero order rejections, zero market-closed attempts, complete volume-balanced journals and no overnight positions or swaps. The unchanged source and installed binary matched their published hashes; both preset switches were checked in native reports.

The hours-only strategy logged two opportunities and took one trade; its existing position blocked the second. The retest strategy armed two breakouts but produced no qualifying confirmation. No trade is an observed result, not a missing report. Four days, one trade and one calendar-week block cannot establish positive expectancy or superiority over random trading. No new coin-flip percentile or confidence claim is made.

## Statistical reporting repair

Both public benchmark engines previously resampled a single weekly block repeatedly if asked to analyze such a short period. That could produce an unjustified zero-width confidence interval equal to the sole observed weekly expectancy. They now return `[null,null]` and `insufficient_weekly_blocks` when there are fewer than two calendar weeks or fewer than two weeks containing trades. Empty weeks cannot turn a single active week into enough evidence. Two active blocks are only a computational safeguard, not adequate evidence of an edge.

Eight new fixture cases cover both engines, bringing the public test suite to 24 passing tests. The trading EA, its parameters, and the eligible multiweek resampling calculation were unchanged. Existing historical benchmark files retain their original source fingerprints and remain available at the original publication commit; their numeric conclusions were not reinterpreted as new validation.
